About Aljada

A Self-Sustained
Community

Aljada, developed by Arada, is a landmark development that redefines Sharjah living. It is a destination where living, working, entertainment, recreation, shopping and culture all come together in one vibrant urban environment — the downtown of new Sharjah.

The community spans 24 million sq.ft. with a 4.4 km retail boulevard, 4 hotels, 3 schools, an education district, 500,000 sqm of prime office space and 165,000 sqm of leasable retail space, with more than 5 km of parks for residents to enjoy.
